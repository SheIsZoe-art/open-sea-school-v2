Bring your questions. Keep your discoveries.

Will my progress move between online and offline?

These versions have separate browser storage. Export your JSON journal from the school and import it in the other version. Export regularly, especially before moving files or updating. Importing replaces the active journal after confirmation.

Is this a complete accredited school?

This is a playful learning resource, with approximate US grade and age guidance. It does not award recognised qualifications or replace all the reading, writing, discussion, assessment and human support of a full education.
