01

Get started in a few minutes

Open OPEN-SEA-SCHOOL.html in a modern browser. This file contains the school: no sign-up, installation or connection is needed to learn.

  1. Choose an approximate age on the campus. You can change it freely.
  2. Choose a subject, then select Play this course in Learning paths.
  3. Read the mission and inspect the model. Move, adjust, connect or construct as requested.
  4. Select Test my creation to check what you built.
  5. Adjust your attempt if necessary. The lobster’s ? can help.
  6. Export your journal from My journal to keep a portable copy.
On a phone or tablet

If you only see a static file preview, choose “open in browser”. A file viewer may display HTML without running its games.

One possible first journey

Campus → age 6–7 → Mathematics → a shell lesson → build → check → ask for an explanation → save a discovery.

02

What the application contains

810playable courses
9 × 9 × 10ages × subjects × courses
25interaction families
80positive jokes

The catalogue has nine age bands, from 6–7 to 14–15, and nine subjects. Each subject/age combination contains ten courses: 90 courses per age band and 81 paths altogether.

Additional spaces include a 16-family arcade, a nine-family puzzle reef, Captain Claw’s cove, a sandpit, six workshops and 24 everyday-life project cards.

How to interpret these numbers

A course is a learning mission with content and success conditions. Several courses share the same interaction family. The catalogue does not contain 810 unrelated software engines or constitute a certified complete school education.

The school and this manual are in English. French examples intentionally remain French in the world-language subject.

05

Complete a course, step by step

1. Understand the brief

The title identifies the course. The mission states its goal and constraints. A marine setting and, in some courses, a live visual model provide context. Check units, existing quantities and limits before acting.

2. Build your response

Your response is the state of the model: placed objects, selected values, painted cells, connected labels or planned commands. Some briefs permit more than one valid solution.

3. Test and improve

Test my creation checks the model. An incorrect attempt leaves your work in place so you can change it and test again. Success displays an explanation and saves a course medal. Using a hint does not prevent success.

The activity meter is not a mark

It reflects interaction with the task. A full meter does not necessarily mean the answer is correct. The check button applies the mission’s actual rules.

4. Explain and transfer

Open How this idea works for an explanation. Take it beyond the screen offers a project using paper, safe materials or discussion.

Use My discoveries to record what you understood, then select Save discovery. Unsaved writing can be lost when leaving the page.

5. Continue

Next course opens the next course in the selected subject and age band. Other courses remain open on the map: there is no compulsory order. A medal records completion of that mission, not lasting mastery of the whole concept.

06

The controls and 25 interaction families

Mouse or touch

Drag a piece into a zone, or select it and then activate the destination heading. Tap or click tiles and cards to select or paint them.

Keyboard

Use Tab to reach controls and Enter to activate buttons. In moving games, select the piece before its destination. Sliders use native browser controls.

Reorder a card

If cards are appended to a construction area, return a card to its supply and add it again at the right moment. This lets you rebuild sentences and timelines.

Captain’s clawful: move a group

In distribution courses that offer it, open Captain’s clawful. Choose From, To and a group size from one to ten. Select Carry this group.

The captain moves no more objects than are available in the source. The tool does not solve the manifest: you still decide the quantity and check relationships and totals.

How difficulty changes with age

Difficulty is not only the number of pieces. Games can change the type of association, the number of constraints, the representations to translate, the required detours or the steps of reasoning.

Approximate ageMemoryNumber gridMaze and collection
6–73 pairs; identical pictures4×45×5; no mandatory collection
7–84 pairs; identical pictures4×45×5; no mandatory collection
8–95 pairs; picture/word4×47×7; no mandatory collection
9–106 pairs; picture/word6×67×7; one required stamp
10–117 pairs; sum/value6×69×9; one required stamp
11–128 pairs; sum/value6×69×9; one required stamp
12–139 pairs; expression/value or fraction/decimal9×911×11; two required stamps
13–1410 pairs; expression/value or fraction/decimal9×911×11; two required stamps
14–1511 pairs; expression/value or fraction/decimal9×913×13; two required stamps

Examples of changing reasoning demands

  • Circuits: a simple loop progresses to several outputs, a red fault to avoid and a switch budget.
  • Distribution: a given amount progresses to ratios, missing quantities or incorrect starting loads.
  • Programming: prepare a route, then respect obstacles and sometimes a command limit.
  • Music: copy a motif, then derive an unprinted bar or follow relative key changes.
  • Composition: place an object, then coordinate spacing, ordering or symmetry constraints.
Adapt without comparing people

These parameters describe designed tasks, not a measurement of an individual child’s abilities. If a game is too easy or too demanding, return to its menu and choose another age. The age references are not school-placement decisions.

Replay: the same mission or a new expedition?

ControlEffectUse it to…
Reset this missionRestore the current mission’s starting configuration.Try the same reasoning again from the beginning.
New expeditionGenerate another supported course configuration.Check understanding with different data.
New missionGenerate another arcade mission.Explore another version of the same game.
Reset this puzzleRestore the current puzzle.Try the same grid and constraints again.

Variation can affect values, ratios, prefilled containers, wording, circuits, sequence rules, obstacles, musical patterns and spatial operations.

Expedition #… identifies the current course setup. It is not an import command or an input for restoring the game elsewhere.

What is not unlimited

Knowledge and wording banks are finite. Shuffling a historical statement does not create a new historical fact. Procedural replay does not replace varied reading, investigation or human discussion.

09

Ask Captain Claw for help

Captain Claw stays visible in a screen corner. Select the ? to open contextual help. The panel offers three degrees of assistance:

  1. Understand the goal: restate or reread what the mission asks.
  2. Choose a method: suggest a manageable strategy.
  3. See an explanation: explain the reasoning or offer a more specific lead.

Next hint advances the help; Simpler returns to an earlier level. A sea joke offers a pun. Close the panel with its cross or Escape. Help does not remove lives or medals.

The captain uses prepared text and game data. It is not an online conversational assistant. It does not receive free-form conversation or contact a remote AI service.

In Captain’s cove

The joke chest contains 80 positive jokes, filtered by the selected age. Another sea joke advances the selection. The free studio lets you change the captain’s hat, conduct a little dance and ask for a kind thought. Those activities have no marks or ticket costs.

Medals, tickets and hidden games

Three different things

ItemMeaningHow to obtain it
Course medalThis particular mission reached a valid state.Complete the course check, with or without help.
Arcade medalA game family has been completed.Complete a mission in that arcade family.
Practice ticketAn entry to certain optional bonus corners.Complete a fresh course expedition; at most 30 tickets can be saved.

A new expedition in a previously completed course can earn a ticket. Resetting and rechecking the same expedition cannot earn a second one. Earlier achievements may provide a small initial credit when the wallet is first created.

Discovery and entry are separate conditions

Discoveries depend on distinct completed courses. Completing one course fifty times does not count as fifty different courses. Some discovered corners then require two or three tickets per entry.

Example: Midnight glow lagoon

Complete ten distinct courses to discover it. Entry costs two tickets. If you had two, entering uses them. You may retry or reset that current puzzle, but another ticketed entry needs more tickets.

What stays available

Courses, earned medals and the separate puzzle reef stay available. There are no cash payments, calendar penalties or daily streaks to lose. A bonus can relock because tickets have been used, not because you were away.

Bonus corners are themed versions of puzzle families. Their learning is not exclusive to the cove: the separate reef remains freely accessible.

The nine puzzle families

Brain-teaser reef does not require tickets. Read the current rule whenever you open a puzzle: it may differ from the previous one.

PuzzleHow to playWhat changes
Shell memoryTurn cards to find matching pairs. Close an unmatched pair when you are ready to continue.From 3 to 11 pairs: identical pictures, pictures and words, expressions and values, or fractions and decimals. The two faces may look different.
Lighthouse towersSelect a dock’s top disc, then its destination. Never put a larger disc on a smaller one.Scrambled starts, changing destination docks, more discs and later neighbour-only transfers. Read the destination rather than assuming it is always on the right.

Check my puzzle checks the result. Some rule tasks move to another round after success: reread the changed rule before continuing.

Not a cognitive assessment

The puzzles practise specific memory, planning, selection and spatial strategies. They do not provide an IQ score, diagnose a condition or guarantee general cognitive improvement.

13

Create, investigate and leave the screen

The sandpit

Choose Rake, Dig, Shell or Castle, then click or touch a point in the tray. Each action places a mark or object. Undo removes the latest mark; Fresh sand clears the tray.

Place selected tool in the centre offers an alternative to pointing. Successive placements shift slightly. The tray supports up to 300 marks.

Sand creations are temporary: they are not included in the journal and are not guaranteed after reloading or closing the school. There is no expected answer, score or medal.

Workshops

Six cross-subject projects include a shell market, a newspaper of good questions, a paper bridge, a dream neighbourhood, a postcard and a shared-knowledge box. Each suggests an activity and a record to produce. They can be printed and are not automatically marked.

Life & Better World

The 24 cards cover repair, reuse, shared books, readability, cooperation, privacy, checking information and small community projects. Each offers an action, reflection and related skill practice.

The purpose is to listen to needs and observe what helps. The cards do not assign “world impact” scores or judge families’ circumstances.

14

Save, export and change devices

The journal stores completed courses, arcade medals, saved discoveries, practice counts and cove tickets locally. There is no personal account or device-to-device synchronisation.

Export a copy

  1. Open My journal.
  2. Select Export my journal.
  3. Keep the JSON file somewhere you can find.
  4. Copy it to a USB drive or other storage if needed.

Import a copy

  1. Open the school on the other device.
  2. In My journal, select Import a journal.
  3. Choose a JSON export from the English edition.
  4. Confirm replacement of the current journal.
Import replaces; it does not merge

Export the active journal first if you want to keep it. Recognised data and format are checked before replacement. Invalid files are refused. Older English journals remain readable; French and English editions use separate formats and storage.

On a shared device

Only one journal is active. Export one player’s work before importing another player’s file. There is no profile selector or password protecting the notes.

What is not a saved game

An unfinished construction, uncompleted command queue, open puzzle or sand mark is not an exportable saved game. An expedition number alone cannot restore those manipulations.

Erase the journal

Erase the journal on this device asks for confirmation before removal. Export a copy first if you want to keep the work.

15

Reading, keyboard, sound and privacy

Reading and listening

Reading + enlarges main text. The captain’s hints are text: full spoken narration of all lessons is not included. An adult or another reader can support younger learners.

Music is generated locally after a music control is activated. Visual scores work without sound. Stop cancels pending notes, and navigation also stops pending playback. No microphone is used.

Alternative controls

Use button labels, Tab and Enter. Sliders are native controls. Moving objects can use selection followed by a destination. Grids often identify rows, columns or colour values in their labels.

These alternatives are designed into the interface. Experience with each browser, screen reader and touch device still needs real-world testing.

Data and connections

The school does not need to send answers to a server and does not ask for an account, camera or microphone. Reference links open external sites and need a connection.

Free-text notes are accessible in the browser and exports. Avoid passwords, addresses or private information. Another person using the same browser on a shared device may access the active journal.

16

Use the school with a family or group

Choose→Manipulate→Explain→Return

Choose a specific idea rather than only collecting medals. Ask the learner to predict before checking: “How many are missing?”, “Why does this card belong here?”, or “What will this button change?”

After success, change one condition or use New expedition. New data can reveal whether the learner understood the idea rather than memorised card positions. If needed, ask for a hint or try another age band.

In a group, rotate reading, building, observing and explaining roles. Off-screen projects offer opportunities to draw, write, talk and test with suitable materials.

Mixed voyages

Start a mixed voyage offers six stops in different subjects at the selected age. Complete a stop and use the next-stop button. You may leave: completed work stays in the journal, but the remaining voyage is not a saved session to resume later.

There is no compulsory daily duration or streak. Choose session lengths and breaks around the learner’s availability and fatigue.

18

Troubleshooting and common questions

I cannot see the latest changes.

Reload the tab and check which file path is open. A copy in another folder does not update when the main copy changes. Export your journal before moving or replacing files.

The file opens but its buttons do nothing.

Open it in a browser that runs JavaScript rather than a static file preview. Extract the ZIP and open the application HTML.

Dragging a piece does not work.

Select the piece, then activate the destination heading. Distribution courses may also offer the Clawful tool for moving groups.

The age setting feels too easy or too demanding.

Return to the menu and change age. These are designed starting points, not individual assessments. Familiarity with the concept matters too.

My circuit fails even though every switch is on.

Read both outputs and the budget. Later models often require green on and red off. XOR means exactly one input is true, not both.

A new game looks like the last one.

Choose New expedition or New puzzle rather than Reset. Some families mainly vary parameters; factual banks remain finite. A new launch does not guarantee a never-before-seen rule.

Why has an available bonus relocked?

Discovery and entry cost are separate. Some corners need tickets for another entry. Complete a fresh course expedition to earn one. Courses and the separate puzzle reef stay open.

I replayed a course but did not receive a ticket.

Resetting the same expedition does not award another ticket. Complete a new expedition. The wallet saves at most 30 tickets.

Sound does not start.

Activate a music control and check device sound. If audio is unavailable in that browser, use the visual score. No audio download is required.

My journal looks empty after moving the HTML.

Local-file storage depends on the browser and file location. Import your last JSON export. Without an earlier export, the manual cannot recover data removed by the browser.

Another player’s journal replaced mine.

The application does not merge journals and supports one active journal. Import your previous export, and export before switching players in future.

A grammatical sentence is refused.

Card puzzles check an intended construction rather than providing a general language checker. Another grammatical order may not be the expected target. Read the constraints and explanation; the application is not judging every possible free-form sentence.

Is this a complete recognised school?

No. It is a learning library with pedagogical references, without accreditation or a diploma. It does not provide every experience, discussion, reading activity or assessment involved in years of schooling.

Scope of the manual and school

This manual describes the local English edition of The Sea University — Captain Claw’s school, with 810 courses and the deeper difficulty revision available on 31 October 2026. Counts and labels refer to that edition.

Selected Common Core and NGSS references inform parts of the application. This is not a certification of complete alignment with a US state curriculum, and age mappings are indicative.

Logic checks have been run on content, generated solutions and game variants. They do not replace trials with children, independent teacher review or testing visual layout, touch, audio and accessibility on real devices.

Schooling also includes sustained reading and writing, speech, physical activity, investigations, human relationships and assessment that this file does not fully supply.
