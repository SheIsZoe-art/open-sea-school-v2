DEEPER DIFFICULTY REVISION — VERIFICATION
31 October 2026

Passed automated checks on the self-contained English school:

- JavaScript syntax, 810 distinct course IDs/titles and ten courses per subject
  per age band.
- 9,720 generated course configurations accept a constructed solution and reject
  a deliberately incorrect one. Every configuration starts unsolved.
- 1,350 generated arithmetic exercises independently recalculated from prompts.
- Slider targets, quantities, annotation indices and routes remain reachable.
- 324 new puzzle configurations across nine families and nine age bands.
- Generated Sudoku grids have one checked completion, at sizes 4, 6 and 9.
- Tower solution paths obey disc ordering and optional neighbour-only links.
- Generated lamp solutions actually turn all lamps off under their toggle rule.
- Maze stamps and exit can be visited through valid corridor routes.
- Rule sets have both matching and non-matching cards; the multi-round UI changes
  the rule after success and completes only after all required rounds.
- Signed token puzzles meet the value, count, space and budget constraints.
- 1,152 arcade configurations across all 16 families and nine age settings.
- Every arcade configuration has a feasible solution and rejects a wrong state.
- A generated rover program was actually executed through its generated maze.
- Structural age checks: representation matching, grid sizes, stamps, multi-resource
  constraints, circuit switch/rule count, multiple recipe ingredients and variables
  on both sides differ between younger and older settings.
- Same-age replay checks: 16 different Sudoku layouts, 16 maze layouts, three lamp
  rules, compound selection rules, varied sentence cases and three circuit topologies.
- Printable cards and companions regenerated from the current course snapshots.

This is document-simulation and logic testing, not a full-browser visual test.
Actual display, drag/touch behaviour, keyboard focus, audio permissions, printing,
downloads and screen-reader behaviour remain to be checked on real devices.
Automatic local-file inspection is blocked by the integrated browser policy;
no workaround was attempted.

Task demands are deliberately differentiated, but empirical difficulty for a
particular child has not been measured. The catalogue still needs teacher review,
learner trials and broader educational material. It is not a cognitive assessment,
certified curriculum or substitute for complete schooling.
