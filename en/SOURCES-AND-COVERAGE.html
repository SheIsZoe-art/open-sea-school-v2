US states and schools differ. Common Core maths/literacy and NGSS inform selected topics, not every course or a universal national curriculum. Some international and French humanities examples remain. French is a world-language subject.

810 playable courses

Ten courses in each of nine subjects and nine age bands. The school uses 25 assessed interaction families, shared controls and course-specific configurations. Quantities, unequal deliveries, initial loads, wording, models and reference patterns vary. Factual banks remain finite.

Broader learning still required

Extended reading and writing, spoken language, rich science investigations, physical education, deeper humanities, mature programming, teacher assessment and many other areas cannot be replaced by this file. No qualification, accreditation or full-school equivalence is claimed.

Cognitive practice

Nine puzzle families offer age-adjusted practice in remembering locations, planning, constraints, switching rules and spatial reasoning. They are not a diagnostic or a guarantee of general cognitive improvement.

Bonus games without calendar penalties

Captain’s hidden corners appear after distinct course completions. Some entries use practice tickets earned from fresh successful expeditions. They relock when tickets are insufficient. No paid access, inactivity deadline, lost streak, removed medal or blocked lesson is used. The separate puzzle reef remains freely accessible.
