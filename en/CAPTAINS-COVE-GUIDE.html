Captain Claw’s cove

80 positive sea, nature and ecology jokes, age-filtered in the school. Two free corners are available immediately. Discoveries appear after 3, 6, 10, 15, 22, 30 and 40 distinct completed courses. Some later entries cost two or three saved practice tickets.

A new successful course expedition earns one ticket, capped at 30 saved. Rechecking a reset copy of the same expedition does not earn another. Tickets are used when entering an optional bonus challenge. They do not expire with inactivity. Course access and earned work are never removed.

Brain-teaser reef

  • Memory pairs: remember positions and compare symbols.
  • Lighthouse towers: use legal moves and smaller subgoals.
  • Coral square: generated unique 4×4, 6×6 or 9×9 grids with row, column and box constraints.
  • Lagoon lights: investigate reversible cross, diagonal or row/column effects.
  • Alternating trail: derive two or three streams with varied steps, directions and distractors.
  • Changing rule: progress from one feature to AND, OR, XOR, negation and conditional rules across several rounds.
  • Rotation mosaic: follow rotations, reflections and compound colour transformations.
  • Route planning: navigate generated mazes and, later, collect mandatory stamps in side passages.
  • Pearl weights: combine values, then coordinate signed values, item count, space and budget.

Puzzles are always open in the separate reef, without tickets. Difficulty varies with the selected age band. They are task-specific practice, not IQ tests or clinical cognitive training.
