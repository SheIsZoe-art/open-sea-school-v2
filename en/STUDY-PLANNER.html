Nine flexible age pathways

This 36-week organiser schedules the available 90 courses per age band. Weeks 1–30 introduce three courses a week. Weeks 31–36 revisit fifteen a week. It is not a complete school timetable or a full year’s teaching hours. Adjust pace, repetition and support to the learner.

US grade hints in the school are approximate. Local programmes and individual development differ. Add wider learning experiences and knowledgeable guidance.
